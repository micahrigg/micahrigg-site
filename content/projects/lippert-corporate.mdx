## Context

As part of Lippert's 2023 company-wide rebrand, the corporate website needed to be
rebuilt on a new platform to match the refreshed brand and give the content team more
control over pages going forward. The site was built in Nuxt.js with Contentful as a
headless CMS.

## Role

I joined the build to help finish out the library of reusable components and connect
them to Contentful's content models. I was also involved in pre-go-live testing and
supported the Lippert content team directly as they built out pages using the new
components. I maintained the site after launch and added new features as they were
requested.

## Constraints

- A large corporate site with many pages, all needing to stay consistent with the new
  brand
- A non-technical content team would be building pages themselves, so components needed
  to be predictable and forgiving to use
- A rebrand timeline that didn't leave room for rework once components were in place
- Component structure had to line up cleanly with Contentful's existing content models

## Approach

I built out the remaining reusable components in Nuxt/Vue and wired each one to its
corresponding Contentful content model, so the content team could assemble pages without
touching code. Ahead of launch, I ran testing passes to catch issues before go-live, and
worked directly with the content team as they built pages, resolving problems as they
came up. After launch, I stayed on as a maintainer, adding new features as the
business requested them.

## Outcome

The site launched on schedule alongside the rest of the rebrand and has been running
in production since, with an established component library that keeps new pages
on-brand and a content team that can build pages independently. I maintained it
through 2026 rather than treating it as a one-time delivery.
