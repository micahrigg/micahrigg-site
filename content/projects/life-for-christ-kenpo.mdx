## Context

Life For Christ Kenpo, a Christ-centered martial arts ministry, was running on an aging
website that no longer reflected the school. The owner wanted the relaunch to coincide
with a meaningful milestone: the school's 25th anniversary.

## Role

I rebuilt the site from the ground up — design, front-end build, and launch — working
against the fixed anniversary date the owner wanted to tie the release to.

## Constraints

- The old site was slow and dated, so performance was a top priority, not an afterthought
- The design needed to work cleanly across phones, tablets, and desktops for a family
  audience that spans a wide range of devices
- The launch was tied to a fixed date — the school's 25th anniversary — which set a hard
  deadline for the rebuild

## Approach

I rebuilt the site on a modern, lightweight stack chosen specifically for load speed,
paired with a responsive layout designed mobile-first so it holds up on any device. The
new design kept the ministry's identity — its Kenpo school branding and Christ-centered
messaging — front and center while giving the site a cleaner, more current look, and
timed the launch to land alongside the 25th anniversary celebration.

## Outcome

The site launched on schedule with the anniversary, giving the school a fast, modern,
fully responsive home online that fit the milestone it was built to celebrate.
