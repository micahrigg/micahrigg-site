## Context

Community Evangelical Free Church needed to replace an aging website that had become
expensive to run and difficult for internal staff to maintain. The congregation serves a
wide range of ages, so the new site had to stay welcoming and easy to navigate for
visitors who are not particularly tech-savvy, while still giving the church office a
straightforward way to keep content current — service times, events like Vacation Bible
School, sermons, and giving information.

## Role

I led the migration end to end: auditing the existing site, planning the move to a
modern, lower-cost platform, rebuilding the design system to stay on-brand, and setting
up the admin experience the staff would use going forward.

## Constraints

- A non-technical staff would be the ones editing the site day to day, so the admin
  experience had to be simple and forgiving
- The visual identity — logo, colors, tone — needed to carry over so the transition felt
  seamless to the congregation
- The audience skews toward a wide age range, so navigation and legibility took priority
  over anything clever
- Budget mattered — part of the point of the migration was to lower ongoing hosting and
  platform costs

## Approach

I rebuilt the site on a modern stack chosen specifically to cut recurring platform costs
compared to the old setup, while pairing it with a content-editing workflow the church
office could use without developer help. The design carried over the existing branding —
logo, green color palette, and photography style — into a cleaner, more legible layout,
with clear calls to action for visiting, giving, and viewing events like the annual
Vacation Bible School program.

## Outcome

The church now runs on a platform that costs less to maintain and is fully editable by
internal staff, with a homepage that leads with a clear welcome, service information, and
current events, and full brand continuity from the previous site.
