While on vacation this summer I started a small side project when nothing else was going on in the evenings. I decided to see how far I could get creating my own SVG generator for laser cutting. I currently have an XTool D1 Pro 20 watt diode laser. It does a great job making quick work of thinner materials such as 3mm plywood or 3mm hardboard. With the extensions I also have plenty of room for larger work pieces. 

## The Idea

I've always thought it would be cool to us 3D printing and laser cutting to build a car someday. The idea came to me during some downtime to see if I could use Claude to make a generator that could output laser cut files for a toy car.

## Why SVG?

SVG is a vector graphic format that works well when imported into laser cutting software. Since it is mostly just math it is also easy to scale and change using a set of parameters.
